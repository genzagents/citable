citable

Can ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity actually read and cite your site? One command tells you, with the fixes.

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/genzagents/citable/main/citable/__init__.py | python3 - yoursite.com

Zero dependencies, one Python file, nothing to sign up for. Or pipx install git+https://github.com/genzagents/citable. Source and README.

Why this one

Most "AI visibility" checkers read robots.txt and stop. But the block that actually keeps you out of answers is usually at the edge: a Cloudflare "Block AI bots" toggle, a WAF rule, a rate limiter. citable fetches your homepage as each AI bot and compares it with what a browser gets. Then it checks the rest: llms.txt, JSON-LD, title and description, whether your text exists without JavaScript, sitemap, canonical. It also tells answer bots (the ones that cite you) apart from training crawlers (which you may block freely).

Weekly watch

A deploy that flips a WAF setting silently removes you from every AI answer. The watch is the alarm: a GitHub Action that checks your sites every Monday, keeps one issue per site up to date, and comments on it the moment an answer bot is blocked, llms.txt disappears, the site goes down or the score drops, which GitHub emails to you. Free, no server, no account. Issue #1 watches this page.

on:
  schedule: [{ cron: "17 6 * * 1" }]
  workflow_dispatch:
permissions: { issues: write, contents: read }
jobs:
  watch:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: genzagents/citable@v0.4.0
        with:
          sites: example.com
